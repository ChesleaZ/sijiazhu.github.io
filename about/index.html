Research Interests

AI for Biology. I am interested in biological foundation models, representation learning, and multimodal generative AI. My work focuses on learning cellular representations from single-cell data and connecting them with biological knowledge and natural language for interpretation.

Statistical Machine Learning. I am interested in high-dimensional inference, scaling laws, and survival analysis. My work examines how data scale and quality shape information recovery from noisy biological data to guide experimental design. I also develop survival analysis methods for clinical studies.
